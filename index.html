FAQ

Planning notes from the current Ridgeview Timberworks site, rewritten for clarity.

When should I contact Ridgeview Timberworks?

Contact Ridgeview Timberworks during budgeting or design whenever possible. Timber trusses and beam packages are easiest to coordinate before framing begins.

What do you need to quote my project?

A final set of plans is best. Rough drawings can help with early budgeting, but final pricing is more accurate when dimensions, quantities, species, finish expectations, engineering requirements, and delivery location are known.

Can Ridgeview Timberworks build structural trusses?

Structural trusses require engineering coordination or stamped truss drawings. Include those documents with your quote request if they are already available.

Is a deposit required?

Because materials are ordered specifically for each project, Ridgeview Timberworks typically requires a deposit when the contract is signed, with the balance due according to the delivery terms.
