FAQ

Common questions before requesting a quote.

When should I contact Ridgeview Timberworks?

Contact Ridgeview Timberworks before framing starts whenever possible. Trusses, beams, and posts are much easier to price and coordinate when they are included in the plans early.

What do you need to quote my project?

A final set of plans is best. For early budgeting, rough drawings and photos can help. For firm pricing, Ridgeview Timberworks needs dimensions, quantities, species, texture preferences, engineering requirements, and delivery location.

Can Ridgeview Timberworks build structural trusses?

Yes. If the project includes structural trusses, send the engineering documents and stamped truss drawings with the quote request. Those documents are required before a firm estimate can be generated.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. Because timbers are ordered and cut for each job, Ridgeview Timberworks typically requires a deposit when the contract is signed.
